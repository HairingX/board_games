## What Danish schools teach when

Fælles Mål 2019 is in force, with its skill and knowledge goals as guidance only since 1 January
2025; new, slimmer _fagplaner_ are planned from the school year 2027/28 ("Andet udkast:
Fagplan for matematik", June 2026: 1.–3. klasse natural numbers, place value, +, −, × and
beginning division by mental arithmetic; 4.–6. klasse fractions, decimals, percent, negative
integers and the order of operations; 7.–9. klasse square roots and powers). Which times table
comes in which year is textbook practice, not curriculum.

Sources: [uvm.dk draft](https://uvm.dk/media/115b23fj/260624-fagplan-for-matematik-juni-2026.pdf),
[Hillerød action plan](https://www.hillerod.dk/media/g3xexusu/bilagsmateriale-til-handleplan-for-matematik.pdf),
[Skoleforeningen year plan](https://www.skoleforeningen.org/media/mg5cwu5o/matematik.pdf).

## What motivates without making children anxious

- **Time** (contested): timed practice works when it is short, on what the child already knows,
  aims at accuracy, and compares with the child's own best ("meet or beat"); comparing children
  with each other demoralises. [IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/Docs/PracticeGuide/WWC-PraxGuide-Elementary-Math-Summary-508c.pdf),
  [EdWeek on Maki et al.](https://www.edweek.org/teaching-learning/do-timed-tasks-really-worsen-math-anxiety/2024/08),
  [youcubed](https://youcubed.org/?p=963).
- **Feedback** (strong): immediate, showing the right answer, and explaining beats right/wrong.
  [Butler & Roediger 2008](https://doi.org/10.3758/MC.36.3.604), [Shute 2008](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654307313795).
- **Mixing and spacing** (strong): mixed problem types and missed tasks coming back.
  [Rohrer et al. 2020](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Study/88770).
- **Difficulty**: about 80–90 % correct is a reasonable target; the "85 % rule" is indirect.
  [Wilson et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6831579).
- **Praise**: specific and about how the child worked, never inflated or about being clever.
  [Mueller & Dweck 1998](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.75.1.33),
  [Brummelman et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797613514251).
- **Rewards**: small positive effects; collaboration with some competition works best;
  expected, task-bound rewards can crowd out a child's own motivation; personal-best goals help.
  No shop, loot or streaks that can be lost. [Sailer & Homner 2020](https://opus.bibliothek.uni-augsburg.de/opus4/frontdoor/index/index/docId/109056),
  [Deci, Koestner & Ryan 1999](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.125.6.627).
- **Siblings at different levels**: never rank raw points across levels; rank by improvement on
  one's own best, or count a shared team total.
- **Three answers** are as good as four or five; distractors from real mistakes teach.
  [Rodriguez 2005](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-3992.2005.00006.x),
  [Gierl et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317726529). Typical mistakes: off by one or
  by ten; the wrong operation; the smaller digit taken from the larger (52−17 → 45); a
  neighbouring product in the same table (7·8 → 48 or 63); left to right instead of the order
  of operations (3+4·5 → 35); tops and bottoms added in fractions; place value with decimals;
  sign errors with negatives; base times exponent (3² → 6). Never "try again among the
  remaining two", which is only elimination.
