# Ninja Adventure Asset Pack

- **Made by:** Pixel-boy (https://pixel-boy.itch.io/) and AAA
- **Where from:** https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack, the free download
  ("Ninja Adventure - Asset Pack.zip", 89 MB), fetched on 18 November 2026.
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (`LICENSE.txt`, as shipped in the pack). Attribution is
  not required but appreciated; the app credits the makers.

## What is kept, and how

- `Actor`, `Items`, `FX`, `Backgrounds` and `Ui`: as in the pack, unchanged.
- `Audio`: every music track, jingle and sound, converted from Ogg Vorbis and WAV to AAC in
  `.m4a`, which every phone plays: music at 80 kbit/s stereo, jingles and sounds at 96 kbit/s.
  Folder and file names are kept.
- Left out: the example and preview images, which no game uses.
